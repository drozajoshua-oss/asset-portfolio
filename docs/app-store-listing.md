## Description (≤4000)
Trovault is the easiest way to identify, value, and organize everything you collect — all in one app.

Just snap a photo. Trovault's AI instantly identifies your item, estimates its condition and value, and backs it up with real-time prices from live eBay listings. Then it's saved to your personal vault, where you can watch your collection's total value grow over time.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Scan & identify — point your camera at almost any collectible and get an instant ID
• Real market values — value estimates backed by live eBay listing prices
• Build your vault — every item saved to your private, synced collection
• Track your worth — see your collection's total value, breakdowns, and top items
• Multi-angle scans — combine several photos for a more accurate identification

WORKS ACROSS CATEGORIES
Coins • Sports & trading cards • Watches • Art • Jewellery • Sneakers • Wine • Vintage cars • Comics • Stamps

WHY TROVAULT
Most apps only handle one category. Trovault is the single home for your whole collection — whatever you collect — with instant AI identification and real marketplace pricing in one place.

PREMIUM
Upgrade to Trovault Premium for unlimited scans, live market values, full portfolio analytics, and collection export.

Values shown are AI- and market-based estimates for informational purposes only and are not formal appraisals.
