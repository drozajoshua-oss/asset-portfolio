## Description (≤4000)
Ever wonder what your collection is actually worth?

Most people have no idea — and the few tools that exist are confusing or only cover one thing. Trovault fixes that. Snap a photo of almost any collectible and instantly see what it's worth, backed by real, live eBay market prices — then keep your whole collection in one place and watch its total value over time.

Whether you're a lifelong collector or you just inherited a box of coins, cards, watches or jewellery and have no idea what any of it is worth, Trovault gives you a fast, honest answer.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Snap a photo of your item.
2. Trovault's AI identifies it and estimates its value.
3. See real prices from live eBay listings — not a wild guess.
4. Save it to your private vault and track your collection's total worth.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Scan & identify — point your camera at almost any collectible for an instant ID
• Real market values — estimates backed by live eBay listing prices you can tap through to
• Build your vault — every item saved to your private, synced collection
• Know your worth — see your collection's total value, categories, and top items at a glance
• Add anything — scan it or add items manually, with your own custom categories
• More photos, more accuracy — combine several angles for a sharper identification

WORKS ACROSS EVERYTHING YOU COLLECT
Coins • Sports & trading cards • Watches • Art • Jewellery • Sneakers • Wine • Vintage cars • Comics • Stamps — and anything else, with custom categories.

WHY TROVAULT
Specialist apps only handle one category. Trovault is the single home for your entire collection — whatever's in it — with instant AI identification and real marketplace pricing in one place. It's the easiest way to finally know what you have, and what it's worth.

PREMIUM
Upgrade to Trovault Premium for unlimited scans, live market values, full collection analytics, and export. Start with a free trial, then $4.99/month or $39.99/year (just $3.33/month). Cancel anytime.

Values shown are AI- and market-based estimates for guidance only and are not formal appraisals. For high-value items, confirm with a qualified appraiser.
