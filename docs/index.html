Common questions

How accurate are the values?
Values are AI- and market-based estimates for guidance, built from live eBay listings. For high-value items, always confirm with a qualified appraiser.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone. You can also use "Restore purchases" inside the app.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Dashboard > gear icon > Delete account. This permanently removes your account and collection data.
