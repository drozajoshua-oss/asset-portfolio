## Posting notes
- **Best times (UAE account, global audience):** 5–7 PM GST weekdays hits both
  EU evening and US morning. Consistency matters more than the exact hour.
- **Stories:** reshare each post to Stories the same day with a link sticker
  to trovault.io — Stories get the tap-through, the grid builds credibility.
- **After launch:** pivot the same rhythm to user-story content ("what my
  followers' items were worth") — screenshots of real scans are the best ads.
- Keep captions in your own voice — edit freely; the hook (first line) matters
  most, it's all people see before "more."
